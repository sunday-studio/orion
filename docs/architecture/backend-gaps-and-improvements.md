# Orion Backend Gaps and Improvements

Status: read-only audit, 2026-10-14, `main` @ `810ac82`. Nothing in the product tree was modified.
Scope: `apps/node/`, `apps/core/`, `deploy/`, `Makefile`, `.github/workflows/`, `docs/node-core-contract.md`.
Companion: [backend-architecture.md](backend-architecture.md) (flowcharts, data model, topology).

Severity: **Critical** = the product fails at its core job or data is exposed by default; **High** = exploitable or silently wrong in realistic conditions; **Medium** = reliability, scale or hardening gap; **Low** = hygiene.
Effort: **S** under a day, **M** one to three days, **L** more than three days.
Every finding was confirmed by reading the cited code. Items that depend on runtime behaviour I did not execute are marked "inferred".
